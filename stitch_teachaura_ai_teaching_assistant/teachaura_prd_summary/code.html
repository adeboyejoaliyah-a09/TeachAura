Core Functional Modules

grid_view

1. Teacher Dashboard & Overview

Command central providing today’s scheduled classes, weekly syllabus pacing against NERDC standards, streak tracking, and instant access to lesson staging.

auto_awesome

2. AI Lesson Preparation & Notes Studio

Extracts syllabus objectives to generate full teacher lesson notes, pupil exercise book short notes (2B notebook format), low-cost local experiment ideas, and step-by-step chalkboard layouts.

slideshow

3. Guided Teaching Mode (Live Classroom Flow)

A timed 45-minute live stage runner (Introduction 5m, Concept 15m, Activities 10m, Evaluation 10m, Wrap-up 5m) with responsive teacher controls: start, pause, extend, skip, classroom cold call randomizer, and chalkboard projector display.

quiz

4. Assessment Studio & Question Generator

On-demand generator for Continuous Assessment (CA) and term examinations featuring multiple-choice, fill-in-the-gap, theory questions, and step-by-step marking schemes calibrated to WAEC and BECE grading standards.

calendar_month

5. Weekly Teaching Planner & Progress Tracker

Dynamic redistribution of syllabus coverage around unexpected disruptions (public holidays, midterm breaks, sports days) paired with milestone badges and positive consistency streaks.
