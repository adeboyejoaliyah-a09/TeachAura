SECTION A

Multiple Choice Questions (10 Marks)

[1 Mark Each]
1.

During normal inhalation (breathing in) in humans, which of the following mechanical events occurs simultaneously?

A Diaphragm relaxes and moves upwards, ribs move downward
B Diaphragm contracts and flattens, ribcage moves upwards and outwards check_circle
C Thoracic cavity volume decreases, lung pressure increases
D Intercostal muscles relax, forcing air out through the trachea
verified
Marking Scheme Key: Option B 1 Mark

NERDC Competency Spec: JSS 2 Theme 2 Topic 3 - Mechanics of Breathing. Award full 1 mark for Option B. Explanatory note: Contraction of the diaphragm increases thoracic volume, which reduces internal air pressure, drawing atmospheric air into the lungs.

2.

The microscopic clustered air sacs in the human lungs where gaseous exchange takes place between air and blood capillaries are known as:

A Bronchioles
B Alveoli check_circle
C Trachea
D Pleural membranes
verified
Marking Scheme Key: Option B 1 Mark

Examiner Guidance: Note that Bronchioles are fine branches, but only Alveoli possess thin-walled single-cell epithelial surfaces for diffusion.

3.

Study the anatomical diagram of the human thoracic respiratory system below and identify the organ labeled [X]:

A The Right Bronchus
B The Muscular Diaphragm check_circle
C The Pericardium
D The Esophagus
verified
Marking Scheme Key: Option B (Diaphragm) 1 Mark

WAEC / BECE Standard: Diagram interpretation tests student ability to identify the convex muscle separating thoracic from abdominal cavities.

2.

(a) Define the term cellular respiration and write the word equation representing aerobic respiration.

(b) Name two common respiratory ailments prevalent in metropolitan areas in Nigeria and state one preventive measure for each.

[Total: 5 Marks]
Mark Breakdown: (a) 2.5 Marks • (b) 2.5 Marks Difficulty: Balanced Cognitive (HOTS)
Part (a) Scheme:

• Definition (1 Mark): The biological process through which glucose is broken down within cells using oxygen to release energy (ATP).

• Word Equation (1.5 Marks): Glucose + Oxygen → Carbon Dioxide + Water + Energy (ATP). (Deduct 0.5 if ATP/Energy is omitted).

Part (b) Scheme:

• Ailments (1 Mark): Asthma, Bronchitis, Pneumonia, or Tuberculosis (0.5 mark each for any two named).

• Preventive Measures (1.5 Marks): Avoiding heavy industrial smoke/dust masks, ventilation, immunization/BCG vaccine (0.75 mark each).
